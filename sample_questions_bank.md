# Sample Questions Bank

Here is a list of sample questions you can use to test the different AI skills in the Lenny Growth Assistant:

## Skill: General Q&A
*Use these questions when the "General Q&A" skill is selected to test factual retrieval.*

1. What are the biggest mistakes early-stage startups make when trying to scale their growth, according to Casey Winters?
2. How does Gokul Rajaram suggest you design your product development process?
3. What are Elena Verna's 10 growth tactics that never work?
4. What does Gustaf Alstromer say about working with 600+ YC startups?
5. What is the 'tar pit' idea according to Dalton Caldwell?
6. Explain the concept of product-led growth (PLG) versus sales-led growth.

## Skill: Atomic Essay Writer
*Use these prompts when the "Atomic Essay Writer" skill is selected to test content generation and formatting.*

1. Write an atomic essay about how to achieve hypergrowth in your business based on Carilu Dietrich's insights.
2. Write a highly opinionated essay about when and how a startup should hire its first Product Manager.
3. Write an essay contrasting the core differences between a PM and an Engineering Manager.
4. Write an essay about how a product leader can successfully navigate the transition from managing products to managing people.
5. Write an essay summarizing the biggest mistakes early-stage startups make when scaling, based on Casey Winters.
